# Blue Sky classroom media · Release 87

Release 87 updates the illustrations and word recordings used by the existing Grade 5 and Grade 6 curricula. The media map replaces the previous bundled URLs in both fresh and saved lesson settings. Teacher-uploaded recordings and explicit decisions to use speech synthesis or omit a picture still take precedence. Previously created rooms can still load their original media URLs.

The update covers 324 picture references and 318 recording references across 387 configured entries, including the legacy Grade 6 profile. The build includes 273 WebP illustrations and 267 byte-for-byte MP3 files. Images come from the Blue Sky elementary teacher flashcards, with a maximum display dimension of 384 px. Recordings come from the publisher's 2024 [Grade 5 Word List](https://digi-keirin.com/es24/eigo/24eigo5/24eigo5a_10801_x.php) and [Grade 6 Word List](https://digi-keirin.com/es24/eigo/24eigo6/24eigo6a_10801_x.php).

The selected recording must match the prompt, including differences in tense and articles. For example, the past-tense lessons use the past form of *read*, while *a cat* retains its existing recording. Entries without a matching resource use their existing assets. The local reference catalogue retains the original artwork, recording filenames, and per-entry source URLs.

## 日本語

小学校5・6年生の既存カリキュラムで、単語のイラストと音声を更新しました。旧設定を含む387項目のうち、イラスト324件と音声318件の参照先を更新しています。教材のイラストは最大384ピクセルのWebPに変換し、Word ListのMP3は元のファイルを使用します。

先生が追加した録音、端末の読み上げ設定、画像を使わない設定は引き継がれます。教材に一致する素材がない項目は従来の素材を使用します。例えば過去形の *read* には過去形の録音を使い、*a cat* には冠詞のある従来の録音を使います。
